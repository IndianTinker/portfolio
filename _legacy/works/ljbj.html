GENERATIVE POSTER DESIGN

Walk Ljubljana

The city of Ljubljana came up with a poster competition to encourage walking in the city. This project was my entry.
How to encourage people to walk more ? Discover and Make Stories.

Walking has always been one of my favorite activities to explore a city. I feel, it embeds you in a city much deeply compared to an isolated metro or a trapped bus. On a recent trip to Ljubljana with Salik, I was amazed to explore the city on foot. It is a very walkable city (less ups and downs, good scenes, river, and sidewalks).

One day, while my usual competition and work search, I found an interesting competition around joys of walking. I thought it would be a nice opportunity to explore my love for walking with generative art on a poster medium.

I recalled my time in the city and tried to remember streets and various stories associated with them. It was not very difficult as I maintain an online log of my 'psychogeographies' of a place. These stories, I feel can only be discovered by a more open and exploratory travel rather than instagrammer and checkbox travels.

So, I compiled these stories on a map. For the streets, I did not got a chance to go, I used the words : 'Walk' to fill the lines. The weight of the text is in proportion to the street width data from map (Mapbox).

In these posters, I want to show the acts of making and discovering stories using a map metaphor.
