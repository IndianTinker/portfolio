DATA VISUALIZATION

Student exchange visualizer

IED Spain has many partner universities. Students needed a way to access this information visually to decide and apply for a university.
Where can I go on an exchange as an IED student? The project can be explored here.

A video demonstrating the project.

The aim was to design and develop a geographic visualization based on an evolving dataset of IED's partner universities. The platform needed to be simple and easy to maintain. I worked on transforming this data from a google sheet file. First step was to 'forward geocode' the university names. Then the entire dataset was converted in a geojson object which was used to render the webpage using MapboxGL.js

The user clicks on a college or searches any city she would go on an exchange and the corresponding data of the university is displayed.
The updates can be performed by updating the googlesheet and reloading the dataset generated from it. The project can be explored here.

This project was developed as a part of my internship at IED Digital Design Lab, Madrid. I worked on this from design to development with contributions from Lara and Carlos.
